Integrated modeling and optimal design of antagonistic SMA joints

Four coupled models describe how design choices affect bending, stiffness, twist, and output force.

I developed a modeling framework that predicts bending angle, load–deflection stiffness, distal twist, and output force from a common set of thermal inputs. It links joint geometry and wire behavior to these four design objectives. I tested the predictions on an optimized prototype and compared its performance with two non-optimized designs.
