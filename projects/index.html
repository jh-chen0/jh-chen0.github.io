Projects

Robotic instruments for minimally invasive surgery, from miniature bending joints to image-guided interventions.

Transoral surgical robotics

An adjustable bending section and a compact wrist help a surgical instrument reach and grasp tissue in the larynx.

Active cooling for SMA joints

Pre-cooled air flows through a flexible sleeve to shorten the time a miniature surgical joint needs to cool.

Modeling and joint optimization

Four coupled models describe how design choices affect bending, stiffness, twist, and output force.

Handheld biopsy needle driver

A reusable handheld drive separates from the disposable biopsy needle and supports three distinct needle motions.

Robotic ultrasound and 3D reconstruction

This ongoing project uses RGB-D surface imaging to plan a transabdominal ultrasound scan with a UR5 robot.

A steerable pericardial robot

A small variable-stiffness wrist allows the tip of a percutaneous robot to bend after insertion.
