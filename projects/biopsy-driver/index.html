A handheld needle driver for endoscopic ultrasound-guided biopsy

A reusable handheld drive separates from the disposable biopsy needle and supports three distinct needle motions.

I designed and prototyped a handheld needle driver for endoscopic ultrasound-guided fine-needle biopsy, with transgastric sampling of splenic lesions as the target application. The reusable drive accepts a disposable needle through a quick-release interface. Its controller provides rapid puncture, controlled-rate advancement, and reciprocating sampling to match different stages of the surgeon’s workflow.
