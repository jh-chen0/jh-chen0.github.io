A hybrid robotic instrument for transoral laryngeal surgery

An adjustable bending section and a compact wrist help a surgical instrument reach and grasp tissue in the larynx.

I developed a shape memory alloy (SMA) joint whose bending length adjusts continuously from 20 to 40 mm. It bends up to 70° in either direction. I integrated this joint with a 4 mm tendon-driven pitch–roll–yaw wrist in a seven-degree-of-freedom instrument. The instrument reached the vocal cords without neck extension in a human head phantom and an ex vivo porcine head. In the porcine model, it also grasped tissue from multiple directions.
