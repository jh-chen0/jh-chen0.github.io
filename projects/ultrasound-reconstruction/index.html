Robotic ultrasound scanning and 3D reconstruction of the bladder and prostate

This ongoing project uses RGB-D surface imaging to plan a transabdominal ultrasound scan with a UR5 robot.

I am developing abdominal surface reconstruction and scan-path planning from RGB-D images for a UR5 robot. I am also implementing force feedback and admittance control to regulate contact between the ultrasound probe and the abdomen. The goal is to improve the repeatability of bladder and prostate shape and volume assessments.
